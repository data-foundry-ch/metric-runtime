# Conference talk

## Your Dashboard Is Too Late

metric-runtime originated from ideas demonstrated in the PyData talk
**“Your Dashboard Is Too Late”**, using the fictional **PyPizza** company and
the **Great Lunch** promotion incident.

The talk shows why dashboards that only calculate and display metrics arrive
too late for action — and what happens when metrics become executable semantic
objects with dependencies, detection, state, ownership, and graph-aware
investigation.

## Flagship example

The conference demo lives on as the open-source flagship example:

→ [examples/pypizza/](../examples/pypizza/)

Local speaker scripts, slide notebooks, and brand assets are gitignored
presentation material and are not part of the published library surface.

## Positioning

its own for developers who never saw the talk.
